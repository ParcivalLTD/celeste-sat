    How to read this
    
    
    Each frame is one step of a C port of Celeste's published Player.cs, brought up to the current game (v1.4) with the changes in the official changelogs and checked against the community TAS (normal, climb and dash states, 60 fps, single-precision floats in the game's order of operations). A beam search finds a fast route first. CBMC then asks the SAT solver whether any input sequence leaves the room one frame sooner. "No" is a proof over every possible input sequence of that length, within the model. When the first frames of the route are held fixed, the proof covers every continuation from that point.
